Purpose of this guide

Why this exists

Most project management advice is written for large corporates with dedicated PMOs, enterprise software and full-time project managers. Irish small businesses and charities rarely have any of that — instead they have a manager wearing five hats, a handful of staff or volunteers, a tight budget, and a funder or board asking for results.

This guide translates MSc-level project management theory — drawn from bodies of knowledge such as PMI’s PMBOK® Guide (PMI, 2021) and the APM Body of Knowledge (APM, 2019) — into short, practical, Irish-contextualised sections you can act on this week.

Who it is for

Built for two audiences

  • Small and micro businesses in Ireland running client projects, product launches, or internal change with limited resources.
  • Charities and community organisations delivering funded programmes, often with volunteer boards, restricted grants, and Charities Regulator obligations.

If you are managing a project without a project management qualification — this guide is for you.

Accessibility notes

This guide is written in plain English at roughly a general-reading level, set in Atkinson Hyperlegible — a typeface designed specifically to increase legibility for readers with low vision and some forms of dyslexia. Every page keeps a single accessible colour palette, provides text alternatives for every video and audio script, uses accordions and tables instead of dense paragraphs, and supports keyboard navigation with a visible focus outline throughout.
